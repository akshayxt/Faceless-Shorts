Three Little Kittens: How Mittens Keep Your Fingers Warm! — Three little kittens lost their mittens and they began to cry! But wait, do mittens make heat, or do your fingers do all the work?
Kids science answer for "three little kittens rhyme for kids" with fun 2D animation by BabyBeats Rhymo!

Mittens don't produce any heat on their own. Instead, fluffy wool fibers trap tiny pockets of still air, creating an insulating blanket that keeps your body's natural heat from escaping into the cold winter air.
