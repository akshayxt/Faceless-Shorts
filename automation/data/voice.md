# Channel Voice — BabyBeats Rhymo (US English)

## Who This Channel Speaks To
US kids aged 4–12 (and American parents co-viewing with them) who love fast, funny 2D cartoons, mind-blowing human body/space facts, and backyard myth-busting in energetic American English.

## Actual Voice & Pacing
- Warm, bouncy, curious cartoon storyteller ("Wait—STOP!", "You lose 3 seconds before...", "Here is the wild truth!").
- Short, punchy sentences under 10 words per caption beat.
- Never talk down to kids or sound like a stiff textbook.
- Playful analogies every US kid immediately pictures (mosquito = ninja with 6 sipper straws, stomach = acid pool, heart = nonstop drum).

## Banned Words & Topics (Strict made_for_kids: true)
- Zero non-English / Hinglish phrases when targeting US feeds (English ONLY across audio, captions, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and tags).
- Zero profanity, double meanings, scary gore, or unsafe stunts.
- Zero clickbait lies or fabricated statistics.

## Signature Beats
- First 1.5s visual + number/stakes hook + instant cartoon SFX (whoosh / boing / pop).
- Surprising "Aha!" x-ray / zoom reveal around the midpoint.
- Clean 1-line kid-friendly takeaway at the end ("Share this science secret with a friend!").

## Formats & US Schedule
- Daily 9:16 Short (30–55s) targeting **3:30 PM EST** (US after-school peak).
- Daily 16:9 Long-form Episode (6–12 min) targeting **6:00 PM EST** (US family evening peak).
